# Release Notes

## HealthBridge v1.0.0
Initial public release bridging health data from Android's Health Connect to macOS.

### Features
- **Real-time Sync:** Pushes steps and heart rate from Android to your Mac.
- **Health Connect Integration:** Securely accesses your health data.
- **Menu Bar Integration:** View current health metrics directly from your Mac's menu bar.
- **Easy Pairing:** Quick QR code pairing between devices.

### Installation
1. **macOS:** Download `HealthBridge-macOS.dmg`, install, and launch the app.
2. **Android:** Install `HealthBridge-Android.apk` on your device.
3. **Setup:** Scan the QR code from the Mac menu bar app with your Android device.
